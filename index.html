Fujitian

A constructed language with its own sounds, grammar, vocabulary, and writing system.

What is Fujitian?

Fujitian is a constructed language, or conlang: a language deliberately created rather than one that developed naturally over centuries. It has its own rules for building sentences, forming words, and expressing ideas.

Fujitian is more than a code that replaces English words. Its grammar and sentence structure give it a distinct identity.

How does Fujitian work?

  • Sentence order: Fujitian generally follows a subject–complement–verb pattern, unlike the more familiar English subject–verb–object order.
  • Descriptions: Adjectives and adverbs usually follow the nouns or verbs they describe, while determiners appear before nouns.
  • Verbs: Verb forms express grammatical information, and subject pronouns can be omitted when the subject is already clear.
  • Pronunciation: Distinct letters and diacritics mark different sounds. For example, ç represents an “s” sound, ș represents “sh,” and ř represents a rolled “r.”

Writing system

Fujitian can be written using a Latin-based spelling system with additional letters and accent marks. It also has a custom script with original letterforms, developed to give the language its own visual appearance.
